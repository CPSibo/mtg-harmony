# Domain

What the app models and the words the code uses for it.

## Every card is a token

The app supports games of Magic: The Gathering built around
[Discord, Lord of Disharmony](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/798/discord-lord-of-disharmony), which repeatedly casts
copies of random cards. Copies of spells are tokens, so every card the app tracks is a token: either one of those
copies, or a physical card a player mirrors onto the board by hand. Tokens that leave the battlefield cease to
exist, so the app has no library, hand, graveyard or exile. A card is either on the board or gone.

## Glossary

| Term                    | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Code                                                    |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fetcher**             | Pulls random non-land paper cards from Scryfall, one at a time, so the player can cast or skip each. Keeps a queue of cards fetched ahead; cards it has shown are in the event log, and "previous" steps back through them.                                                                                                                                                    | `features/discordFetcher`                               |
| **Cast to graveyard**   | Casting the On-deck card without putting it on the board, for what doesn't stay, such as instants and sorceries. It's logged as cast, with `toGraveyard`, and has no stack. There's no graveyard zone: the name only says where the card went.                                                                                                                                 | `castToGraveyard`                                       |
| **Add card**            | Manual search for a specific card, mainly to mirror a physical card onto the board. Filters by regular cards, tokens and extras (emblems, dungeons, the Monarch and the like); regular cards only by default.                                                                                                                                                                  | `features/addCard`                                      |
| **Board** (battlefield) | The pan-and-zoom canvas holding every stack. Positions can snap to a grid.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | `features/battlefield`, `Battlefield`                   |
| **Board card**          | One token: its Scryfall data, which face is showing, whether it's tapped, and its modifiers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | `BoardCard`                                             |
| **Flip over**           | Turning a board card face down. A double-faced card shows its back face; any other card shows the standard card back.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | `BoardCard.flipped`, `cardFaces.ts`                     |
| **Flip 180°**           | Turning a Kamigawa flip card (Scryfall layout `flip`) upside down. Offered only on those cards.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | `BoardCard.turned`                                      |
| **Room**                | A card with two doors (Scryfall: both faces typed Room), each locked or unlocked. Casting one to the board asks which door opens, or both; closing the question leaves both locked, as for a Room that enters without being cast. A locked door is dimmed under a padlock (named beside the card when shown as text), and Card details locks or unlocks each door. Not logged. | `BoardCard.lockedDoors`, `isRoom`, `RoomDoorsModal.vue` |
| **Meld**                | Combining two meld halves (Scryfall layout `meld`, such as Bruna and Gisela) into the card they meld into, which takes the first half's place. Offered only while both halves are on the board; it fetches the melded card from Scryfall, so it needs a connection. **Unmeld** splits a card melded on the board back into its two halves, each as it was.                     | `meld`, `unmeld`, `BoardCard.meldedFrom`                |
| **Create token**        | Putting a token a card makes (Scryfall's related tokens, such as Mitotic Slime's Ooze) on the board in a stack of its own, from Card details. One token is created at once; several open a picker showing each token's name, type line and power/toughness. Logged with the card that made it. Needs a connection.                                                             | `createToken`, `tokenParts`, `TokenPickerModal.vue`     |
| **Landscape** card      | A card whose showing face is printed sideways: battles, Rooms, classic split cards, planes. It enters lying sideways, and tapping turns it 90° clockwise to upright.                                                                                                                                                                                                           | `isLandscape`                                           |
| **Stack**               | A group of board cards that sit and move together at one board position. Every card on the board belongs to exactly one stack.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | `BoardCardStack`                                        |
| **Primary**             | The card a stack is built around. A card on its own is the primary of a one-card stack.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | `BoardCardStack.primary`                                |
| **Attachment**          | A card attached to the primary, such as an Aura or Equipment. Detaching it puts it back on the board as its own stack.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | `BoardCardStack.attachments`                            |
| **Under**               | A card physically placed under the primary: exiled "until this leaves the battlefield", hideaway, and similar effects. Drawn as edges peeking out above the primary. Put a card under with **Put under...**, take it out with **Take out**.                                                                                                                                    | `BoardCardStack.unders`                                 |
| **Modifier**            | A counter (+1/+1, loyalty, ...) or granted keyword (flying, haste, ...) on a board card, with a count. The available list is in `types/MtgConcepts.ts`. A card whose text says it enters prepared starts with **Prepared** (`entersPrepared`).                                                                                                                                 | `BoardCard.modifiers`, `MODIFIERS`                      |
| **Exploding** a stack   | Splitting a stack so each of its cards becomes its own stack.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `explodeStack`                                          |
| **Find on board**       | Searches the cards on the board (name, type line and text, on every face; every word must match), steps through the matching stacks top to bottom, and pans to and selects the current one. Board only, never Scryfall.                                                                                                                                                        | `useBoardFind`, `FindBar.vue`                           |
| **Selection**           | The stacks picked to act on together. A plain tap selects one stack; Ctrl/Shift-click, selection mode or a box add more. Selection commands in the palette and the selection bar act on it.                                                                                                                                                                                    | `useSelectionStore`                                     |
| **Widget**              | A feature's registration: the commands and settings it contributes to the command palette and settings menu.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | `features/widgets`                                      |
